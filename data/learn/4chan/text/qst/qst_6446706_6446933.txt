>>6446706 > Herd of Xi'Ni > *The Tilanki long roamed The Glittering Road (most other sapient species would have a different name for the nebula stretching across the upper section of the Galaxy.) At first, they wandered blindly, their base instincts causing them to group together for safety, and to carry their calves with them. When The Eldest reached out to the Mother's with his mind and convinced them to carry his words to their children, and the First Herd was formed. * Boons: > Leviathan Brains - The Tilanki due to their size and the immense calculations they must make to move through space and not starve, have increased Psi production from population when well fed. > Xi'Ni's Gospel - The Herd of Xi'Ni are blessed with an advanced communications network formed from metal, Psi, and Neutronium, allowing near instant broadcasts of the Eldest's speeches across the nebula and other star systems if a properly equipped Herd is there. Malice > Herd-bound - The Mothers and Bulls of the Herd cannot leave the nebula, so great is their size that they must constantly be consuming from the Glittering Road, calves can go on brief jaunts outside the nebula to scout for new food sources (gas giants, life containing planets). The Herd of Xi'Ni do not conventionally settle planets, instead they breed Herds that carry buildings upon themselves for their young and industry, and strip planets for materials without concern for habitation.
