Grand Margrave of Beeleji >Species: Raptorian : Feathered Avians with four eyes, two on each side of their head and a beak filled with sharp teeth. Intelligent, capable of flight in atmosphere but not in void. Boon takes both: Grand Realm - fully controls multiple star systems >For centuries raptors have wondered what lay out there in the black. They created an empire out of nothing then expanded it to new and distant stars. Encountering nothing they considered intelligent, it left only themselves the undisputed masters of the galaxy. Malice - apathetic collapse. Spirit is set to 0. (Spirit is akin to patriotism and collective willpower. Population cannot reproduce.) >There is no Marquis of the Margrave. Their royal line has ended. Not by assassins, warfare, disease or design rather by lack of interest in copulation. A condition now widespread across its population. An Eternal Castellan now rules in the interim, trying desperately to move all hearts but to no avail. These once mighty flock now slumbers perhaps never to awake again.
