>>6446706 Nation name: The Qharyx Lore: The Qharyx are a silicon based species whose bodies are composed of living crystal, metallic veins and self growing mineral structures rather than flesh and blood. Their ancestors are believed to have first emerged within the cooling ejecta of a shattered protoplanet where immense heat, pressure and exotic minerals gave rise to the first living crystal colonies. Light and electrical impulses flow through intricate crystal lattices in place of nerves, while their memories are stored within their ever changing internal structure. They communicate through harmonic vibrations, pulses of light and electromagnetic resonance making their language sound like the ringing of crystal to other species. Their civilisation is as alien as their biology. The Qharyx do not construct cities or starships they cultivate them growing living structures that continue to adapt long after they are created. New individuals slowly form from mineral rich growths over decades and damaged bodies gradually regenerate by growing new crystal. To the Qharyx order and harmony are the natural destiny of the univers, and their society is devoted to shaping the stars themselves into lasting, enduring structures. Boon: Quantum Lattice Memory - The memories of every Qharyx become embedded within vast planetary crystal archives after death. Future generations can resonate with these archives, drawing upon the knowledge and experiences of countless predecessors. Boon: Living Crystal Matrices - Their cities, starships and computing systems are grown rather than manufactured. Once established, they continually regenerate damage adapt to new conditions and improve over time without requiring complete reconstruction. Bane: Alien Cognition - The Convergence struggles to understand species that think on short timescales.
