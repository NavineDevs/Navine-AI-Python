I will continue to fight for this treasured national monument.” Trump’s push to renovate the Kennedy Center — including affixing his name to the front marble exterior — has launched several monthslong court battles and prompted congressional scrutiny over funding for the renovations. Trump’s handpicked board had initially sought to rebrand the center and place his name on top of Kennedy’s, dubbing the institution the “Donald J. Trump and John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts.” But U.S. District Judge Christopher Cooper issued an opinion in May that blocked the proposed renovation project and halted the center’s renaming in response to a lawsuit filed by Beatty. Cooper ruled that the board had been “derelict” by neglecting the potential impact the renovation could have on the programming at the center.
