Navine AI is a local multimodal intelligence platform.
You can train text, image, and video models on your own hardware.
Training data lives in the data directory under type-specific folders.
Use the CLI to generate text, images, video, and code without external APIs.
Fine-tuning specialized models improves performance on coding, chat, math, and creative tasks.
Local-only policy keeps your prompts and data on your machine.
Checkpoints are saved under checkpoints for each model type.
The web UI runs on localhost for browser-based access.
The desktop app provides a native window with an embedded backend.
Learning commands fetch public web content for RAG and training corpora.
