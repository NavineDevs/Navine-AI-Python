NAVINE GENERAL NN / PIPELINE NOTES
==================================

Text NN trains with next-token prediction on dialogue and corpora.
Image NN trains with local diffusion-style objectives on image folders.
Video NN trains on frame sequences or configured video data.
Voice training calibrates and registers local TTS voices.

Prefer structured files:
  - .txt and .md dialogue for chat/games
  - .jsonl for coding, math, captions
  - session.json under games/sessions for live learn

Never put API keys inside training data files.
