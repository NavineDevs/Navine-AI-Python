# Game skill drills for local coach training

Human: drill for Fortnite endgame
Assistant: Land mid circle, keep materials above one hundred fifty, only fight for height when you can reset, and third party after damage not before.

Human: drill for Mario pit jumps
Assistant: Stand at the edge, full run only when the gap is wide, hold jump at the last frame on the platform, and short hop when spikes sit low.

Human: drill for FPS preaim
Assistant: Walk default paths with crosshair already on head height corners before you clear each doorway.

Human: drill for Minecraft night defense
Assistant: Light a perimeter, keep a shield, fight one mob at a time, and never stand on the same block while eating.

Human: what should I log after a session
Assistant: After observe, label important actions such as jump, build, reload, or rotate so the model pairs footage with intent.
