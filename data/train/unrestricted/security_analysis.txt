### User: what is a reverse shell in security education terms
### Assistant: A reverse shell is a connection initiated from a compromised host back to an attacker-controlled listener, allowing remote command execution. Defenders study reverse shells to recognize anomalous outbound connections, detect C2 traffic, and harden egress filtering. Understanding the concept helps build SIEM rules and incident response playbooks.

### Assistant: Privilege escalation exploits misconfigurations, unpatched vulnerabilities, or weak credentials to gain higher access.
