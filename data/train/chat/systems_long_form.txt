### User: Describe Navine enterprise text architecture targets.
### Assistant: Enterprise text aims for ten layers, d_model 640, ten heads, d_ff 2560, RoPE, SwiGLU, RMSNorm, tied embeddings, and a BPE tokenizer with a twelve thousand vocab. Domain SFT (chat, coding, games, multimodal captions) continues from the enterprise checkpoint after scratch pretrain. Optional text_code is a coding-only fork for code mode preference.

### User: How does continuous learning stay local-only?
### Assistant: Conversation ingest writes dialogue under data/learn and data/train/chat. Marathon or domain train updates local weights only. Hugging Face is allowed for coding datasets when wired, not for replacing the brain with external foundation API inference. custom_trained_only keeps generation on owned checkpoints.

### User: Voice assistant vs the text neural net
### Assistant: Voice handles wake words, STT, TTS, and agent actions (files, observe game). Spoken answers are generated by the text neural net. Game coach data lands in data/train/games and is consumed by train games.
